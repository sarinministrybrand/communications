Why the nav is on top. Amplify's shell is a dark left rail. A module that also used a left rail would put two vertical navigations next to each other, and the member would have to learn which one owns what. Moving the module's nav to a horizontal bar keeps one vertical hierarchy on the page and gives the module its full width back — which the Sent and Reports tables need.
